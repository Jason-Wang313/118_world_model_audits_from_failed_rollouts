# Literature Map

## Crowded Areas

- World models and latent dynamics for control.
- Probabilistic model-based reinforcement learning and latent MPC.
- Robot manipulation benchmarks and large robot datasets.
- Scalar uncertainty, ensemble disagreement, conformal risk, and active probing.
- Causal confusion and mechanism-level intervention reasoning.
- Sim-to-real and domain randomization.

## Local Novelty Boundary

The paper should claim a planning-facing mechanism audit from failed rollouts. It should not claim a new generic world model, a new large robot benchmark, or real-world deployment robustness.

## Evidence Needed For Final Main-Conference Readiness

- Real robot or accepted high-fidelity simulator transfer.
- Replayable failed-rollout logs with calibrated contact/camera/state metadata.
- Released trained checkpoint or policy/world-model artifact.
- Independent baseline implementations.
- Full manual related-work synthesis.
