# Literature Map

## Crowded Areas

- World-model diagnostic benchmarks.
- Action-conditioned physical prediction.
- Robot manipulation benchmark suites.
- Scalar uncertainty, ensemble disagreement, and conformal risk filtering.
- Active probing and failure-classification repair policies.

## Local Novelty Boundary

The paper should claim a mechanism-localized failed-rollout audit for robot planning. It should not claim a new foundation model, a new generic world-model benchmark, or a universal physical reasoning evaluator.

## Evidence Needed For Final Main-Conference Readiness

- Transfer to an external benchmark or high-fidelity simulator.
- Real robot failures with replayable logs.
- Manual related-work synthesis with accepted benchmark baselines.
- Stronger ablations tying each mechanism label to downstream repair choices.
